# Quiz — Unit VI: Collision Detection & Resolution

**Course:** Legacy of InFest  
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Total:** 10 points  

---

## Questions

### 1. AABB Overlap (2 pts)
Rect A: (x=10, y=10, w=20, h=20)  
Rect B: (x=25, y=15, w=15, h=15)  
Do these rectangles overlap? Explain using the AABB overlap test.

### 2. Axis Separation (2 pts)
Describe the axis-separating theorem (SAT) for AABB collision resolution. Why is resolution order important?

### 3. One-Way Platforms (2 pts)
How does a one-way platform collision differ from a solid tile collision? What condition must be met for the player to pass through from below?

### 4. Hitstop (1 pt)
What is hitstop and why is it used in action games?

### 5. Knockback (2 pts)
Given a player at position (100, 100) hit by an enemy at (80, 120) with knockback_force=150, calculate the resulting knockback velocity vector.

### 6. Collision Layers (1 pt)
Why might a game use multiple collision layers (e.g., player vs enemies, projectiles vs terrain)?
