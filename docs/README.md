## What Changed, In One Paragraph

Legacy of InFest is an **individual** project (one student → one Stage or Boss, selected in Class 1), not a team or multi-stage-per-student project. The course runs **11 classes of 4 hours** (2h theory + 2h practice) plus a 12th Invenio Fest session — not a 16-week schedule. Evaluation uses six **official** instruments (Quices 15%, Prácticas de laboratorio 20%, Evaluación Práctica I/II/III 15% each, Invenio Fest 20%). John is the son of a mining businessman; Jin is the daughter of a fishing-industry family; La Pepita comes from **Crucitas**; La Perla comes from a **rare marine coral**. The ancestral culture is officially named **Tilawa** (fictional), not the real-world Maleku people. The repository places `engine/`, `framework/`, and `stages/` under `src/`, and adds `student_templates/`. El Gavilán Camionero Mascarero is confirmed as a **project-defined** boss filling a syllabus-acknowledged gap (Zone 3 boss "pendiente de definición"), not an official syllabus boss — it is preserved, not removed.

**Nothing was deleted.** The framework architecture, Stage 0, the zone/stage structure, the Enemy Roster, the full Boss Roster (all four bosses), the Asset Bible, and the entire FilterTools/VisionTools/PatternRecognitionTools processing pipeline are confirmed aligned with the syllabus and remain completely intact.
